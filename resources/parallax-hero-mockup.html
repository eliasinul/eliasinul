Why this approach is the intelligent middle path.

Each layer shifts at a different rate using transform: translate3d(), which the browser composites on the GPU — so scrolling stays smooth even on mid-range phones, and the whole thing works fully offline with no WebGL. Displacement is kept deliberately small (a few dozen pixels between layers); heavy parallax reads as amateur and can cause motion discomfort, so restraint is the point.

Built in: the entire effect freezes automatically for anyone with “reduce motion” enabled (try toggling it in your OS settings and reloading) — the scene simply becomes a clean static illustration. That accessibility fallback is what separates an intelligent hero from a gimmick.

Swap these schematic SVG shapes for a hand-drawn or rendered energy landscape and you keep the exact same performance profile. This mockup exists so you can judge the motion before committing to final artwork.
